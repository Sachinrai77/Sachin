01 — About

About Me

A little bit about my journey and what I do.

SR

I'm a BCA (General) student at Poornima University with a strong interest in web development, UI/UX design, and front-end technologies. I recently completed a 3-month UI/UX Design internship at LinkStatic Techno, a specialized static ISP and proxy service provider.

During my internship, I designed user interfaces and improved user experiences for web-based proxy and streaming services. I created wireframes, prototypes, and user flows using Figma, and collaborated with the development team to ensure design feasibility and smooth handoff.

I'm passionate about building clean, accessible, and user-friendly interfaces. Currently based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.
